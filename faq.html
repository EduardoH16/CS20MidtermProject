FAQs

  • What are your hours?

    We're open Tuesday through Friday from 7am - 2pm and from 8am - 3pm on Saturday and Sunday. We're closed on Mondays.

  • Do I need a reservation?

    No, walk-ins are always welcome. For catering and parties, we recommend calling ahead.

  • Do you have vegetarian or vegan options?

    Yes! Several items on our menu are vegetarian, and we have a few vegan options too. Just ask a staff member.

  • Is there parking nearby?

    There is street parking on our block and a public lot about two minutes away.

  • Can I book L’ éléphante for a private event?

    Yes. Send us a message through the Contact page with your date and group size, and we'll get back to you.
